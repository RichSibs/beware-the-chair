Beware The Chair

An ultrarunning game for iPhone. Pace yourself, fuel well and keep moving through the night, because the chair is always waiting.

Plays fully offline. No account, no ads, no tracking and no data collected.

About the game

Beware The Chair is a simulation of the highs, lows and stubborn decisions of ultramarathon racing. Manage your pace, nutrition and energy across long races, keep your rivals in sight, and push on when every part of you wants to sit down.
