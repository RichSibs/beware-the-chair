Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 November 2026

This policy covers the iPhone game Beware The Chair (the "app") and this website. Both are made by Rich Sibley ("I", "me"), an independent developer based in the United Kingdom. I am the data controller for any personal information described here.

In short: the app collects nothing. It has no accounts, analytics, ads, tracking or third-party code, and it never connects to a server of mine. Purchases are handled by Apple, not by me.

What the app collects

Nothing. The app does not ask you to create an account or sign in, and it never asks for your name, email address, location, contacts or any other personal details. The game plays fully offline. The only time it goes online is to ask Apple's App Store for prices and for which purchases you own (see below); it sends no data to me or to anyone else.

What stays on your iPhone

Your race records, stars, settings, runner design and a note of which legs you have unlocked are saved only on your iPhone, so you can carry on where you left off. None of it is sent to me or anyone else, and none of it identifies you. Deleting the app deletes this data. Depending on your settings, it may be included in your own iCloud or computer backups, which you and Apple control.

In-app purchases

Extra legs can be unlocked with a one-time in-app purchase. These purchases are made, charged and kept on record by Apple through your Apple Account. I never see your name, email address, Apple Account or payment details. The app only asks Apple, on your iPhone, which purchases your Apple Account owns, so it can open those legs. Apple gives me sales reports that do not identify you.

Because your purchases are held by Apple, not by me:

  • To get them back on a new or reset iPhone, sign in with the same Apple Account and tap Restore purchases in the game's Settings.
  • For refunds, use Apple's Report a Problem page. I cannot issue refunds or look up your purchases.
  • Apple's handling of this information is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Saving and sharing your result card

When a race ends you can share a result card. Sharing only happens when you tap SHARE and choose where it goes, through the standard iOS share sheet. If you choose Save Image, iOS asks for permission to add photos to your library. The app can only add the card. It cannot see or read your photos.

Ratings

The app may occasionally show Apple's standard "rate this app" prompt. Ratings and reviews go to Apple, and I see them only as they appear on the App Store.

Crash reports from Apple

If you have turned on Share With App Developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may send me crash reports and usage statistics that do not identify you. I use them only to fix problems. You can turn this off at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements. See Apple's App Analytics & Privacy page.

Fonts and other content

The app uses only fonts built into iOS, and every picture, model and sound ships inside the app. Nothing is downloaded while you play, so no other company learns your IP address from the game.

This website

This website uses no cookies, analytics, tracking, advertising, embedded videos or web fonts. Every file, including the stylesheet and images, loads from this site, and text uses your device's own fonts. The site is hosted by GitHub (GitHub Pages). As with any web host, GitHub automatically logs technical information about visits, including visitors' IP addresses, to keep the service secure. I do not receive or see these logs. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

If you email me

If you email me, I receive your email address and anything you include in your message. I use it only to reply and to fix the problem you report, which is a legitimate interest for both of us. I do not add you to any mailing list or share your email with anyone. I delete emails once they are no longer needed to deal with your question.

Your rights

Under UK and EU data protection law (the GDPR), you have the right to ask what personal information I hold about you, and to have it corrected or deleted. In practice, the only personal information I could hold is an email you have sent me. To make a request, email the address below. If you are unhappy with how I handle your information, you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or to your local data protection authority.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. Purchases go through Apple, so Apple's parental controls, including Ask to Buy, apply.

Changes to this policy

If the app or this website changes in a way that affects your privacy, I will update this page and its effective date before the change is released.
