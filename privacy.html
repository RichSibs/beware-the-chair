Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 November 2026

This policy applies to the iPhone game Beware The Chair (the "app"), made by Rich Sibley ("I", "me").

In short: the app does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information.

Information the app collects

None. The app does not ask you to create an account, sign in or provide a name, email address, location, contacts, photos or any other personal details.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress, settings and scores are saved only on your device so you can pick up where you left off. This information is never sent to me or to anyone else. If you delete the app, this saved data is removed with it. Depending on your iOS settings, it may be included in your own iCloud or computer backups, which are controlled by you and Apple.

Internet connection

The game is designed to work fully offline. It does not send any data over the internet.

Analytics, advertising and third parties

The app contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party code or services. Nothing in the app tracks you across other companies' apps or websites.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, I will update this page and change the effective date above before the change is released.
