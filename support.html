Support

Something not working, or got a question about Beware The Chair? Get in touch.

Email: info@bewarethechairultra.co.uk

I aim to reply within a few days.

When you email, please include

  • Your iPhone model and iOS version (Settings > General > About)
  • The app version (shown in Settings > Beware The Chair)
  • What you were doing when the problem happened, and a screenshot if you can

Frequently asked questions

Does the game need an internet connection?

No. Beware The Chair plays fully offline.

Do I need an account?

No. There are no accounts or sign-ins.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, on your device. Deleting the app also deletes your saved progress, so it is worth keeping your iPhone backed up if you want to move to a new phone.

What data does the game collect?

None. See the Privacy Policy.

The game is running slowly or the screen is stuttering

Close other apps, restart the game, and check that Low Power Mode is off, as it can reduce performance. If it still happens, email me with your iPhone model.
